The cozy formula

Three light sources at different heights, two textures (knit and bouclé) and one gentle scent. That’s it.

Questions people ask

How do I make my living room cozy in winter?

Use warm, low lamps instead of overhead lights, add a knit throw and textured pillows, and bring in a gentle scent.

Is a candle warmer safer than a candle?

It has no open flame, which many people prefer around kids and pets. Still keep it out of reach, since the top gets warm.

Do heated blankets use a lot of electricity?

They generally use far less than heating a whole room. Check the wattage on the product listing.
