From the editors

Welcome to the Winter Issue.

Hommely started in 2021 as a small home-goods business in Loretto, Ontario. Talking to customers taught us that the most useful thing we could offer wasn't another product. It was a straight answer: is this worth it, who is it for, and what should I know before I buy?

So Hommely is now a magazine. Each issue brings stories, how-tos and gift guides for real Canadian homes, plus the small finds that make everyday life easier. We read the reviews, compare the options and tell you what we'd buy ourselves.

This issue is about getting ready for the long, cozy season: slowing down, making room, and finding gifts people actually use. Pour something warm and have a look around.

04

Worth it / Skip it

Honest verdicts, before you spend.

Not everything viral deserves a place in your home. Here's how we decide.

Worth it

  • An instant-read thermometerOne undercooked turkey costs more than the thermometer. HF-K10 →
  • Clear storage you can see intoWhen you can see what you have, you buy fewer duplicates. HF-O01 →
  • Rechargeable over battery-poweredHand warmers, lighters and lights you charge by USB pay for themselves. See the fixes →

Skip it

  • Single-job gadgetsIf a good knife already does it, the gadget will live in a drawer.
  • Organizers bought before measuringMeasure the drawer or shelf first. Then buy once.
  • Decor bought for one seasonChoose pieces that work from November to March, not just December.
