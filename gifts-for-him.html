Match the gift to his routine

The commuter: key finder or hand warmers. The homebody: smart mug or smart speaker. The organized one: the charging station.

Add a handwritten line like “For your early-morning coffee”. It turns a useful gift into a thoughtful one.

Questions people ask

What do you get a man who has everything?

Something that upgrades what he already uses daily: a smart mug for his coffee, a key finder for his keys, a charging station for his nightstand.

What’s a good gift for him under $50?

Key finder tags, rechargeable hand warmers or a 3-in-1 charging station are all in that range at most retailers.

Are smart gadgets hard to set up?

Most connect through one phone app in a few minutes. Check that the device supports his phone (iPhone or Android).
